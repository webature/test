01 — The Brief

Built for weather.
Finished for the street.

Hunter takes its cues from the working outerwear of the American west and the wet-weather gear of the North Atlantic — then strips both back to a London-ready silhouette. Every hardware piece is solid, not plated. Every seam is load-bearing. Nothing on the rack is decorative.
